# Strivacity

> THE AI IDENTITY COMPANY

Strivacity is an identity security company that helps brands deliver trusted digital experiences by managing customer, partner, and AI agent identities in a single product. It brings identity orchestration, authentication, consent management, and fraud prevention together to reduce friction, improve conversion, and give teams clear visibility into where identity drives or blocks growth. Delivered as a single-instance SaaS product, Strivacity supports data residency and consistent performance at scale. Leading brands use Strivacity to acquire, convert, and grow customers while maintaining trust, control, and security across every interaction.

## Who this is for

- Security, IT, and development teams responsible for customer authentication (SSO, MFA, FIDO2/passkeys) who want policy-based changes without coding customer-facing UI.
- Product management and customer experience leaders who need branded, low-friction registration and sign-in journeys, including passwordless and social login.
- Marketing teams that need customer consent management and identity experiences aligned to brand messaging and communications.
- B2B and B2C organizations running identity providers and adjacent systems (for example Okta, Ping Identity, CRM/CDP) looking for integrations measured in minutes, not months.
- Organizations with data residency requirements that need to choose a deployment region and operate in an isolated, secure environment per brand.
- Organizations preparing for AI agents to act on behalf of customers or partners and needing delegation, consent, and audit built in from the start.

## How it works

1. Low-code deployment: implement customer identity experiences in days, not months.
2. Start from pre-built scenarios, including passwordless, select MFA methods, and choose when to step up security.
3. Add social login providers with a few clicks.
4. Configure branding and messaging with full control over customer-facing experiences.
5. Use policy-based updates that automatically update customer-facing UI with no coding required.
6. For agentic use cases, register AI agents as their own identity, capture delegated consent, and scope access before any agent action is permitted.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does Strivacity do?**
Strivacity provides a customer identity and access management (CIAM) product covering secure registration, sign-in, consent management, fraud detection, and, increasingly, governance of AI agents acting on a customer's behalf.

**Who is Strivacity best for?**
B2B and B2C organizations that need secure, branded customer sign-in journeys, spanning marketing, product, IT, security, and development teams. It also fits organizations with data residency requirements and those preparing to govern AI agent access.

**How is Strivacity different from legacy IAM vendors or other CIAM products?**
Strivacity uses a low-code, clicks-not-code approach to reduce engineering effort, offers single-instance architecture with data residency by default, and includes agentic identity governance as a standard part of the product rather than a bolt-on.

**What does Strivacity charge?**
Pricing is based on monthly active users (MAUs), with all capabilities included in one product for one cost. See https://www.strivacity.com/products/pricing.
